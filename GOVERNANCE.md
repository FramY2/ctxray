# Governance

CtxWise uses a maintainer-led, contribution-friendly model.

- Maintainers merge changes after tests, documentation, security, and product
  invariants pass.
- Significant schema, privacy, pricing, or compatibility changes should begin as
  a public issue or design discussion.
- Decisions favor user safety, measurement honesty, local operation, and Codex
  compatibility over feature count.
- Maintainers may appoint contributors with a sustained record of high-quality,
  respectful work.
- Releases use semantic versioning and documented changelog entries.

If maintainers disagree, the change remains unmerged until the relevant evidence
or a narrower reversible design resolves the disagreement.
