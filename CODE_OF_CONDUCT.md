# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CtxWise welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private prompts, credentials, and account data.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and avoid misleading performance claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained
disruption, or publishing another person's private information is not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through the hosting
platform. For sensitive security-related reports, use private vulnerability
reporting. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made
without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
